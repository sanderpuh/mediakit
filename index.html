The Story

From bedroom bench to international start gates.

Background

It started in 2018 with a scratched-up toy quad, a bruised ceiling fan, and a determination to fly the same line twice. By the end of that year I'd built my first real 5″ racer on a soldering iron I was still terrified of.

By 2020 I was lapping local pilots at the club field. My first National Series entry ended with a rookie title, and the pattern hasn't changed since: show up, fly clean, take the box.

Today the calendar spans four continents, DCL and FAI campaigns, and a race setup I build and maintain myself — airframe, stack, antennas, and all. The next chapter is 2027: a top-3 World Cup campaign with partners who get what consistency is worth.
